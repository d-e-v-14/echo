# Echo — User Policy

**Effective date:** 12 September 2026

This User Policy sets out the rules for using the **Echo** platform ("Echo" or the "Service"), operated by **IEEE Computer Society VIT**, VIT University, Katpadi, Vellore - 632014, Tamil Nadu, India ("we," "us," "our"). It supplements the **Terms of Service**. The **Privacy Policy** explains how we handle personal information, and the **EULA** covers the app license.

By using Echo, you agree to follow these rules. Violations may result in server-level action by operators (such as kick or ban), removal of content, or account action by the Echo team, depending on the tools available.

---

## 1. Purpose

Echo is a community messaging platform used by the VIT student community with **`@vitstudent.ac.in`** accounts. These rules exist to keep servers usable, reduce abuse, and outline how we enforce the community standards.

---

## 2. Who this applies to

- members, admins, and owners of servers;
- users of direct messages and friends;
- anyone who shares invites; and
- anyone who submits content or contact forms.

If you create a server, you are responsible for how that space is run within the tools Echo provides (roles, channel access, invites, kick, ban).

---

## 3. Eligibility and identity

- You must follow the age and account rules in the Terms (you must be at least 18).
- Do not impersonate another person, organization, or the Echo team.
- Do not share your password or attempt to access another account.
- Usernames and display names must not be grossly offensive or used to impersonate.

---

## 4. Servers, invites, and membership

### 4.1 Invites

Invite links grant access to a server until they expire, reach their use limit, or are deleted. **Treat invites like keys.** Do not post invites publicly if you intend a private community. Do not harvest or brute-force invite codes or server IDs.

### 4.2 Leaving

You may leave a server if you are not the owner. Owners must transfer ownership or delete the server first. After leaving, you may need a new invite to return, and you may not rejoin while banned.

### 4.3 Roles and channels

Owners and admins may create roles, assign or remove roles, and restrict channel access by role. Do not attempt to bypass channel permissions.

### 4.4 Kick and ban

Permitted operators may kick or ban members. A ban prevents the banned user from joining that server while it is in effect.

---

## 5. Messages and user content

You are responsible for everything you send, including text, images, documents, GIFs, mentions, replies, bios, avatars, and server names and icons. Attachments and avatars may be available at public URLs, so do not upload confidential material.

### 5.1 Reporting

If you are being harassed or see something that violates these rules, you can:

- **Report** the user from their profile — choose a category and, optionally, a reason. The report (your account, the reported user's account, and context) is sent to the Echo team for review. You do not have to reveal your identity to the person you report.
- **Hide messages** — from a user's profile, hide their channel messages from your own view within a server. The hidden user is not notified.

### 5.2 Spam and rate limits

Do not flood channels or APIs. Abuse of rate limits may result in action against your account.

---

## 6. Prohibited content and conduct

You must not use Echo to:

1. **Harass, threaten, stalk, or bully** any person, or incite others to do so.
2. **Sexually exploit or endanger minors.** Child sexual abuse material is strictly prohibited. Echo is not for children, and we will cooperate with law enforcement if we become aware of such material.
3. Post **illegal** content under applicable law (including India's IT Act and other criminal laws), or content that promotes violent crime.
4. Share **non-consensual intimate imagery** or others' private personal data ("doxxing").
5. Post **malware**, phishing, or content designed to compromise accounts or devices.
6. **Infringe copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property.** GIF content remains subject to its provider's terms.
7. **Spam**, run scams, or send unsolicited advertising or repetitive DMs or friend requests.
8. Evade kicks, bans, or account restrictions by creating new accounts.
9. Interfere with the Service (attacks on the API, sockets, or infrastructure; scraping at abusive scale).
10. Use Echo for **emergency services** or impersonate emergency authorities.
11. Upload content you do not have the right to share.

**Hate speech.** Do not attack others on the basis of identity (for example, race, religion, nationality, sex, sexual orientation, or disability) in a harassing manner. **Terrorism and violent extremism.** Prohibited. **Sexually explicit or graphically violent content.** Do not post it in servers; direct messages must still comply with law and this Policy.

---

## 7. Friends and direct messages

Friend requests must be sent in good faith. Do not coerce acceptance or use rejected requests to harass. DMs are stored on our servers and are accessible to the participants and, where necessary for operation or law, to the Echo team.

---

## 8. Enforcement

### 8.1 How to report

Use the in-app **Report** action (profile screen), or the **Contact Us** form in Settings, or email **tech.ieeecsvit@gmail.com**. The Echo team reviews reports and takes action where appropriate, such as warning, restricting content, disabling accounts, or helping server operators kick or ban. Legal emergencies should first be reported to local authorities.

### 8.2 Actions we may take

Depending on the situation, Echo or server operators may:

- remove or restrict content;
- warn, kick, or ban from servers;
- invalidate invites;
- disable accounts; or
- preserve data for legal process.

Enforcement may be asymmetric — operators have more tools than ordinary members.
