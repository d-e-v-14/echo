## 2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide, information generated by your use of the Service, and limited information from services you choose to connect.

### 2.1 Account and profile information

When you register with email and password, we collect:

- email address;
- a hashed password processed by **Supabase Auth** (we do not store your plaintext password);
- username, display name, and (on email sign-up) date of birth;
- optional biography, avatar, and status; and
- an account-creation timestamp.

When you sign in with **Google**, we receive a Google ID token and may use your Google email, subject identifier, name, and profile picture (as an avatar). Google Sign-In and email sign-up are limited to **`@vitstudent.ac.in`** accounts.

### 2.2 Authentication and session data

We process short-lived access tokens and refresh tokens, and store session values securely on your device. On logout, we clear locally stored tokens and unregister the device from push notifications.

### 2.3 Servers, membership, roles, and invites

If you create or join a server, we store server names and icons, ownership and membership records, roles and permissions, channel and category configuration, invite records (including expiry and use limits), and kick/ban records related to your participation.

### 2.4 Messages and user content

We store the content you send or upload, including:

- server channel messages and direct messages (text, attachments, replies, and mention records);
- attachments in supported formats, stored in **Supabase Storage** with public URLs;
- GIFs chosen through our GIF picker (your search queries are sent to Giphy);
- friend requests and friendships;
- contact-form submissions (name, email, message);
- **reports** you submit about another user (see Section 2.5); and
- **message-hide preferences** you set (see Section 2.5).

### 2.5 Reporting and message hiding

**Reports.** When you report a user, we collect the report: your account, the reported user's account identifier and username, the server and channel where relevant, a category (for example Spam, Harassment, Hate speech, Impersonation, Inappropriate content, Other), the reason you provide, and a timestamp. Reports are stored in a `user_reports` record and reviewed by the Echo team.

**Message hiding.** When you hide a user's channel messages in a server, we store a viewer-only preference pairing your account, the hidden user, and the server. The hidden user is not notified, their messages remain visible to other members, and this is not a global block.

### 2.6 Device permissions

Depending on your choices, the App may request permission to use your camera, photo library, microphone, notifications, and clipboard, for the purposes shown in the system prompts (for example, taking or selecting photos and receiving message alerts). We do not read your contacts book, and we do not collect precise location data.

We do not use advertising identifiers, run in-app advertising, or sell personal information.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- create and authenticate accounts;
- display profiles and status to other users as designed;
- operate servers, channels, roles, invites, and moderation tools;
- deliver, store, and display messages, attachments, and notifications;
- maintain friend relationships;
- send password-reset and verification emails;
- respond to Contact form submissions;
- review user reports and act where appropriate;
- honor your message-hide preferences; and
- protect the Service against abuse (for example, through rate limiting).

---

## 4. How we share information

### 4.1 Other users

Content you post in a server channel is visible to members with access to that channel. Direct messages are accessible to the participants in the thread. Invites you share can be used by anyone who obtains the link until it expires, reaches its limit, or is deleted. Avatars, server icons, and attachments are stored with public URLs, so anyone with the URL may retrieve the file.

When you report another user, the report details are shared with the Echo team that reviews reports; the reported user is not told who reported them. Message-hide records are used only to filter your own view.

### 4.2 Service providers

The Service relies on processors including:

| Provider | Role |
|---|---|
| **Supabase** (Auth, PostgreSQL, Storage) | Authentication, database, and file storage (region: `ap-south-1`) |
| **Google / Firebase** | Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android push) |
| **Expo / EAS** | Build, distribution, and push infrastructure |
| **Giphy** | GIF search and content delivery |
| **Redis** | Real-time state and rate limiting |
| **Hosting providers** | API hosting, logging, and backups |

These providers process information on our behalf under their own terms and policies.

### 4.3 Legal and safety

We may disclose information where we believe in good faith that it is required by law, legal process, or a lawful government request, to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users and the public, or to investigate and prevent abuse.

## 5. Storage, retention, and deletion

### 5.1 Where data is stored

Account, message, membership, and file data are stored on Supabase. Real-time and rate-limit state is stored in Redis. Copies of tokens and some state remain on your device.

### 5.2 Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to operate the Service. Messages are retained until deleted by you, by server deletion, or as part of account deletion. Report records are retained for a reasonable period needed to review and act on reports. We retain limited records where required by law or for abuse prevention, security, and troubleshooting.

### 5.3 Account deletion

You can delete your account from the App under **Settings → Delete Account** by typing `DELETE MY ACCOUNT` to confirm. Deleting your account removes your profile and initiates deletion of your associated data, such as messages, attachments, memberships, and owned servers, in line with our retention rules. You may also request deletion by emailing **tech.ieeecsvit@gmail.com** or by using the deletion-request form on our website.

Some information may be retained where required by law, or where needed for legitimate operational reasons such as preventing abuse, resolving disputes, or troubleshooting.

### 5.4 Your choices

You can update your profile (display name, bio, avatar, status) at any time, change your password, leave or delete servers you own, remove your avatar, hide or unhide messages, opt out of notifications, and request account deletion as described above.
