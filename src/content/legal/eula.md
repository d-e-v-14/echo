## 2. What the Software does

Echo is a client for the Echo servers. It provides user interface and local processing for, among other things:

- account registration and login (email/password and Google Sign-In);
- profile and avatar management;
- server, channel, role, invite, and membership management;
- text messaging in servers and direct messages, including attachments and GIFs;
- reporting another user to Licensor for review;
- hiding another user's channel messages from your own view within a server (viewer-only; not a global block);
- friends and mentions;
- optional push notifications;
- secure on-device storage of session tokens;
- a contact form and an account-deletion flow.

The Software communicates with APIs at our configured backend, with **Supabase**, **Google**, **Giphy**, and push-notification infrastructure.
